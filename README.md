# 🎮 PlayAbility Adaptive Software

**PlayAbility Adaptive Software** is a Windows app that lets you **add the controls you’re missing** and **build your perfect controller setup**.

The idea is simple: with just your **webcam** and **microphone**, PlayAbility gives you **face and voice recognition** on top of the peripherals you already use — keyboard, mouse, gamepads, adaptive devices — so you can extend them with **new ways to play** and **fully control every game the way you want**.

👉 **Download the app here:** [https://playability.gg/](https://playability.gg/)
Use it **for free**, with **unlimited access to all features** and **one profile included**.

---

## 🧩 What You Can Do with PlayAbility

### 🗣️ NEW! Voice Recognition in 24 Languages

Say a word or phrase and instantly trigger any action: keyboard keys, mouse clicks, gamepad buttons, analog sticks, or triggers.

---

### 🎯 Control Analog Sticks and Triggers with Your Head and Face

Move your head to steer, aim, or look around, and use facial gestures to accelerate, brake, or fire.
Everything behaves like a **real stick or trigger**, not just digital presses.

---

### 🖱️ Turn Mouse Movements into Game Controls

Convert mouse movement into:

* Analog stick aiming
* WASD-style keyboard inputs
* Gamepad button presses

Perfect for FPS games or when you want mouse precision mapped to controller input.

---

### 😀 Use Facial Gestures as Universal Inputs

Smile, blink, raise your eyebrows — any gesture can become a **button press**, a **trigger**, or even **continuous stick movement**.
PlayAbility recognizes **over 50 different face gestures**, so you can choose the ones that feel most natural to you.

---

### 🧠 Control Your Mouse Hands-Free

Use **head tracking** to move the cursor, and combine facial gestures or mapped keys to click, drag-and-drop, or scroll.
Works for both **gaming** and **everyday PC use**.

---

### 🔗 Unify All Your Gear

Merge adaptive controllers, joysticks, pedals, or any gamepad with PlayAbility’s inputs into a **single, seamless virtual controller**.

---

### ⌨️ Mix Head, Mouse, and Keyboard Inputs

Combine head movements with mouse motion or keyboard presses, and map them to any gamepad action, trigger, or stick.

---

### 🎮 Remap Everything to Game Controls

Reassign keyboard keys, mouse clicks, mouse movement, voice, and face/head gestures to:

* Any gamepad button, stick, or trigger
* Or directly to keyboard presses and mouse clicks

---

### 🌐 Play Beyond Your PC

Control:

* **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox** via **Remote Play**
* **Nintendo Switch** with an **8BitDo Micro adapter**

> And this is just the beginning — something very cool is coming soon 😉
